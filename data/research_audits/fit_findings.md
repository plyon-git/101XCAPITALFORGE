# Operating-capital and equity financier research

Retrieved September 30, 2026 UTC (September 29 evening America/Denver).

89 distinct companies researched; 74 have a source-published business email and normalized US phone. 52 of the complete-contact companies advertise program limits at or above $500,000. None has verified $500,000 currently available cash. None has approved 101XVC or accepted the proposed return/equity terms.

Use `fit_complete_leads.jsonl` for complete-contact CRM imports. `fit_leads.jsonl` also preserves incomplete candidates, clearly marked. `fit_sources/` contains downloaded page evidence and failed-fetch records. Contact publication was checked, not mailbox deliverability or phone response.

The contract-finance group is the first review cohort because the use of proceeds is contract execution and marketing. Most standard operating-capital products require current business revenue, bank deposits, credit history, and sometimes personal guarantees or UCC liens. An advertised unsecured product does not by itself establish approval, non-recourse terms, or willingness to lend against projected closing fees.

The $5M and $115M fee amounts provided by the user are estimates or contingent follow-on amounts, not invoices established by this research. Invoice factoring and supplier purchase-order financing remain conditional alternatives after payment rights and underlying transaction eligibility are documented. Equity candidates are a smaller, separate cohort; debt firms do not automatically offer equity financing.

A published maximum loan amount is a program-capacity signal. It is not current cash, a balance-sheet audit, borrower approval, or a $500K cash-on-hand verification. The dataset records that distinction for every row.

## Product conditions that matter

Funding Pilot, Angle Financial Services, Preferred Funding Group, MACO Financial, and similar credit-based programs warrant separate personal-credit and guarantee review; published startup eligibility must not be translated into 101XVC approval.

Alpha, AMZA, Andij, Vantage Point, TripleCapital, Leading Edge and several similar $500K unsecured LOC pages explicitly rely on an established business and often $1M+ existing annual revenue. Their startup programs commonly have far smaller limits.

Forward Financing publishes $5K-$500K and requires at least one year operating history, $10K monthly revenue and a qualifying owner credit score. Byzfunder advertises up to $500K and at least one year in business with $20K monthly revenue. These are revenue underwriters, not investors in a speculative future fee estimate.

Michigan Family Office publishes a mandate for direct equity in operating businesses and earlier-stage companies, but its preferred $500K EBITDA threshold is not a $500K investment check or available cash. White Pine and LOUD remain incomplete contact candidates because a public telephone was not verified. Decathlon advertises $500K-$15M growth capital, but its email and phone were only found in secondary directories, so the complete-primary-contact list excludes it.

Kapitus's public `info@` email was found in a company-published 2019 financing checklist. That older source is marked in the contact note and should be revalidated before outreach. No email address was guessed from a name or company naming pattern.

No outreach, applications, or contact-form submissions were sent.
