## Funding priorities

Private operating-company investors are the first qualification track when projected closings are the main commercial evidence. Published minority-equity and working-capital mandates are more useful for this request than property-purchase loan directories. They still need to confirm the check and company fit.

Working-capital lenders form a second track if historical receipts, time in business and credit meet published criteria. A future trial projection does not substitute for current revenue. Many useful published products stop below $500,000, so the full raise may require a different approved facility or private investor. Do not assume multiple approvals can be stacked; disclose existing facilities and confirm permitted liens and obligations.

Angel and venture channels are a lower-priority track when their mandate requires a scalable technology company. Internal underwriting software alone does not demonstrate recurring software revenue. Receivables programs require eligible earned receivables; contingent future closing fees are a separate underwriting question.

## Qualification package

Prepare a one-page operating-company description; the original executed agreement and amendment for private diligence; a $100K/state and $500K aggregate use-of-proceeds budget; a weekly cash forecast with realistic first-collection timing; actual financial statements and bank receipts; closing and fallout history; and the proposed debt or equity terms.

The prior primary-equity proposal is $500,000 for 9.3%, approximately $5.376 million post-money before any structure-specific adjustment. Confirm the cap table and definitive rights before putting that offer in investor documents. The older 50% debt-return and 60-day execution inputs are not current default terms.
